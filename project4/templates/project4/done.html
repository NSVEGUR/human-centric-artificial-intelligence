What this study was about

You compared two ways of telling a recommender system what movies you like: picking your favorite of two at a time, and ranking sets of ten. Behind the scenes, each of your answers was used to fit a small mathematical model of your taste (a linear utility over movie features), and the final pairs you saw — on movies you hadn't seen before — were used to check how well each model predicted your real preferences.
