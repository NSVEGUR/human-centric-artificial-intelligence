Before you begin

Movie preference elicitation study — informed consent

What this study is about

You will help us compare two ways of telling a movie recommender what you like: picking your favorite of two movies, and ranking a list of ten movies from most to least preferred. You will try both. The session takes about 10 minutes.

What we collect

Your movie choices/rankings, your answers to a short questionnaire, and timing information. We do not collect your name, email, or any directly identifying information. Data is used only for research on preference-elicitation methods.

Voluntary participation

Taking part is entirely voluntary. You may close this window at any time without penalty; partial responses are not analyzed.

Best on a laptop or desktop

One of the two tasks involves building a ranked list by clicking items in order, which works on any device, but is easiest with a mouse or trackpad.

Questions or concerns

This study is run as part of the Human-Centric Artificial Intelligence course at TUHH. For questions about the study, or to request that your session's data be deleted, contact the study team using the completion code shown at the end.
